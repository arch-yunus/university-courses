# 🌐 Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

> *“İnsan insanın yurdudur; adalet, nezaket ve karşılıklı hürmet aklın süsüdür.”* — **Aliya İzzetbegoviç**

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and high-standard academic environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to making participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

> *“Gerçek bilgi, edeple birleştiğinde medeniyet kurar.”* — **Nizamülmülk**

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive and productive environment include:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and scholarly experiences
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism and technical feedback
* Focusing on what is best for the open educational collective
* Showing empathy, rigor, and generosity towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
* Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a scholarly setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.
